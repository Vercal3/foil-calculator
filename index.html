FOIL Method — A Gentle Way to Multiply Binomials

When two binomials are multiplied, every term in the first must be multiplied by every term in the second. FOIL keeps the four products in order: First, Outer, Inner, Last.

Example: (3x + 2)(x + 4)

First: 3x times x is 3x squared. Outer: 3x times 4 is 12x. Inner: 2 times x is 2x. Last: 2 times 4 is 8. Together: 3x squared + 12x + 2x + 8, which simplifies to 3x squared + 14x + 8.

Why the middle terms matter

The Outer and Inner products are usually the only ones that can be combined. If you skip one, the answer is wrong even when the First and Last products are right. Writing the four products in a line before adding them prevents that mistake.
